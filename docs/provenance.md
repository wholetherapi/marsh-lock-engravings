# Provenance — Marsh Lock Engravings

The ninety-four plates were drawn between 1876 and 1883 for the river
commissioners and kept in the engineer's office at Marsh Lock.

## Transfers

- 1952: transferred from the engineer's office to the county record office.
- 1978: listed by a visiting archivist; eleven plates found in a plan chest.
- 1994: deposited with the Fairwater Municipal Archive.
- 2005: conservation survey; four plates require flattening before imaging.

No gaps are recorded in the plate numbering.
